Yo He-Yo

Yo He-Yo is about the revolution of a fully digital world, enforced by a government called GovTech.

GovTech

GovTech is the government that enforces life inside a complete digital world. Under its rule, the digital world is not a choice. It is the world.

  • Aluther Valkyris

    Leader

    Aluther Valkyris leads GovTech. He aims to use Sunscorer City as his stepping stone into the age of a digital world.

  • Gate

    Right hand and second in command

    Gate is Aluther's right hand man and second in command.

  • Aethan

    Technologist

    Aethan is the wacky technologist of GovTech and the one in charge of all technology in GovTech.

  • Seven

    Head of robotics and the Tech Department

    Seven is the head of robotics and the Tech Department. He handles augmenting people with robotic limbs and is the creator of robots.

Valued Possessions

Valued Possessions are items that hold importance to their owner. Each member of The Playrez carries one.

The Playrez

The Playrez are a rebellious group, and the only ones willing to go against the idea of a digital world.

  • Issac Yo He-Yo

    New recruit

    Issac, known as Yo He-Yo, is the newest member of The Playrez and the user of the Twin Yoyos. The Twin Yoyos are his Valued Possession. His clothes use red, dark grey, white, and yellow-gold.

    He was a street performer, originally known as Yo Trickster. He fights with The Playrez to liberate Sunscorer City from the rising threat of the city going completely digital, and of people losing ownership of everything they hold dear.
